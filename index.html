It all started with
LEGO.

I’ve been building for as long as I can remember. It started with Lego—spending hours putting pieces together, taking them apart, and rebuilding them in ways they weren’t meant to be. It was never just about following instructions. It was about curiosity, experimenting, and figuring things out on my own.

That mindset stayed with me.

Now, I build in a different medium. I’m constantly exploring, trying new things, and pushing ideas further than where they start. I like experimenting—breaking things, refining them, and learning through the process. I’m especially drawn to UI/UX, where the smallest details make the biggest difference—the way something moves, responds, or even pauses.

I don’t just want things to work. I want them to feel right. Clean, intentional, and refined.

For me, it’s still the same process. Just different pieces.
